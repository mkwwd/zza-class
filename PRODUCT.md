# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are Korean web visitors who want to browse and watch short-form drama episodes quickly. Admin users still manage the underlying content library.

## Product Purpose

ZZA presents serialized short videos as a watchable drama library. Visitors browse shows, choose a tape-like title, inspect episodes, and continue watching after login.

## Positioning

The product reframes course and lesson records as short-drama titles and episodes, using a memorable video-shelf selection ritual instead of a conventional catalog.

## Operating Context

The app is a Next.js web app backed by Supabase. Existing course records represent drama titles. Existing lesson records represent episodes. Existing thumbnail URLs remain the visual source when present.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Public visitors can browse published titles. Signed-in users can keep a personal watch shelf. Enrolled courses continue to control access to lesson video pages until the data model is renamed.

## Brand Commitments

The current name remains ZZA. The new surface language is short drama, VHS tapes, a living room TV, and a character choosing a tape from a shelf.

## Evidence on Hand

The project has real Supabase-backed course, lesson, enrollment, and thumbnail fields. No testimonials, commercial metrics, or licensed drama artwork are currently present.

## Product Principles

Make the first screen feel like choosing something to watch, not signing up for a class.
Use existing content truth and avoid inventing commercial claims.
Keep the interaction understandable without requiring motion.
Preserve access control while changing the visible language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The interface must remain keyboard navigable and readable on mobile and desktop. Motion should be decorative and must not hide core actions.
